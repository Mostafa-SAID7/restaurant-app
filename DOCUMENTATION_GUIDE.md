## 🎯 Key Takeaways

### Niche Positioning
- **Primary Niche**: Upscale fine dining in NYC
- **Michelin Recognition**: Central to all messaging
- **Farm-to-Table**: Sustainability and quality focus
- **Culinary Storytelling**: Unique value proposition
- **Theatrical Presentation**: Immersive experience

### Keywords to Target
- **Primary**: Fine dining NYC, Michelin recommended, upscale restaurant
- **Secondary**: Farm-to-table, wine pairing, chef-driven cuisine
- **Long-Tail**: Specific dishes, occasions, dietary preferences

### Content Strategy
- **Phase 1**: Homepage, menu, about page optimization (About ✅ DONE)
- **Phase 2**: Wine page, reservations, FAQ creation
- **Phase 3**: Blog launch with 10 keyword-targeted posts
- **Phase 4**: Authority building and link acquisition

### Success Metrics
- Keyword rankings for primary terms
- Organic traffic growth
- Reservation bookings
- Customer acquisition cost
- Return on investment
