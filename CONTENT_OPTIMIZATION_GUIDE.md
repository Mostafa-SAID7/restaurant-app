## 📖 Menu Page Optimization

### Current State
- ✅ Well-organized by category
- ✅ Price points displayed
- ⚠️ Descriptions could be more keyword-rich
- ⚠️ Missing dietary information prominence
- ⚠️ No chef's notes or sourcing info

### Recommended Enhancements

#### Category Headers (Add Keywords)
```
Appetizers → "Appetizers | Fine Dining Starters"
Mains → "Main Courses | Michelin-Recommended Dishes"
Desserts → "Desserts | Gourmet Finales"
Drinks → "Craft Cocktails | Premium Beverages"
Specials → "Chef's Specials | Signature Dishes"
```

#### Dish Description Enhancement

**Example 1: Wagyu Beef Tenderloin**

Current:
"A5 Wagyu striploin, bone marrow butter, seasonal vegetables and truffle jus"

Optimized:
"Premium A5 Wagyu beef tenderloin, expertly seared and finished with bone marrow butter, seasonal farm-to-table vegetables, and rich truffle jus. A signature dish showcasing Chef Marcus Voss's Michelin-starred expertise. $85"

**Example 2: Charred Octopus**

Current:
"Perfectly charred octopus with smoked paprika aioli, pickled peppers, and micro herbs"

Optimized:
"Chef's Special: Perfectly charred octopus with smoked paprika aioli, house-pickled peppers, and microgreens. A theatrical appetizer that exemplifies NooR's culinary storytelling approach. $22"

**Example 3: Mushroom Risotto**

Current:
"Wild mushroom and porcini risotto with aged parmesan and truffle oil"

Optimized:
"Vegetarian Fine Dining: Wild mushroom and porcini risotto with aged parmesan and truffle oil. Locally sourced mushrooms, farm-to-table ingredients. Perfect for vegetarian fine dining enthusiasts. $36"

#### Add Metadata to Dishes
```
- Dietary tags: [Vegetarian] [Gluten-Free] [Vegan]
- Sourcing: "Locally sourced" / "Seasonal" / "Imported"
- Chef's note: Brief story or inspiration
- Wine pairing: Suggested wine from cellar
- Allergen info: For accessibility
```

### Restaurant Schema
```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "Restaurant",
  "name": "NooR",
  "image": "https://noor-restaurant.com/logo.png",
  "description": "Michelin-recommended fine dining restaurant in Manhattan",
  "address": {
    "@type": "PostalAddress",
    "streetAddress": "[Address]",
    "addressLocality": "New York",
    "addressRegion": "NY",
    "postalCode": "[ZIP]",
    "addressCountry": "US"
  },
  "telephone": "[Phone]",
  "url": "https://noor-restaurant.com",
  "priceRange": "$$$",
  "cuisine": "Fine Dining, Contemporary",
  "aggregateRating": {
    "@type": "AggregateRating",
    "ratingValue": "4.9",
    "reviewCount": "150"
```
